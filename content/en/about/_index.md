Branches Lab explores the next frontier of AI at the intersection of **3D Reconstruction**, **AIGC**, and **Agents**. We build spatial intelligence systems that perceive, generate, reason, and act across digital and physical worlds.

### Research Directions
- 3D reconstruction and scene understanding
- generative world and asset creation
- agent systems with memory, planning, and action
